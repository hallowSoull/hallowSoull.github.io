What is a kitty?

Kittius Monarchias is a curious species of mammal that continues to elude and confuse scientists to this day. It's origin is unknown, only that they came to this earth to grace us with their presence. Several speculate that these suspicious creatures may worship a god of some sort, with many seemingly stumbling upon secret kitty kat meetings and possible sermons. In one such encounter, after the kitties attending the kitty sermon fleed, one of them seemingly dropped a book in their haste to escape. The following text contains excerpts from that very book, possibly lending us a clue to the inner workings of their cute little brains.
