MEME MASHUP

I decided to combine the memes 'Gucci Morty' and 'Guy Pointing at Himself in the Mirror' to create a new meaning of how you feel when you’re barber gives you a good haircut, showing Morty happily staring at himself in the mirror at his new haircut. The mashup takes a more hopeful and wholesome spin on the original 'Guy Pointing at Himself in the Mirror' meme since in that meme the Guy is shown to be very critical of himself while Gucci Morty is seemingly very content with his appearance as suggest by his soft smile. ​
